Dr Sarhan Dental Emergency 24/7 – Homepage Redesign README

1. Website Overview

Business Purpose: Dr Sarhan Dental Emergency 24/7 (https://www.dentist247.ae/) provides 24/7 on-call emergency, general, and cosmetic dental services in Dubai. Operating out of Barsha Heights (Ground floor, inside the 24 Hours Coffee building, 1 Al Seef Street, Al Thanyah First), the practice offers direct patient access to an experienced, DHA-licensed emergency dentist (Dr. Ahmed Sarhan, DHA Licence: 00079324-002, BDS, over 20 years experience).

Target Audience: Patients in Dubai—specifically within Barsha Heights / TECOM and surrounding regions (including Dubai Marina, JBR, JLT, Palm Jumeirah, Business Bay, Downtown Dubai, and Al Barsha)—requiring immediate or scheduled dental care for severe toothaches, root canals, abscesses, broken teeth, knocked-out teeth, wisdom teeth, or lost fillings and crowns.

Homepage Direction: The redesigned homepage establishes a clear, high-urgency operational layout focused on rapid contact and critical service expectations. It emphasizes the direct-to-dentist connection model while explicitly informing users that treatment is by on-call appointment rather than unscheduled walk-in.

2. Main Improvements

Fix 1: Clarified On-Call Appointment Model vs. Walk-In Expectation

Previous Issue: Patients traveling to the physical location risked arriving unannounced, expecting a traditional open walk-in reception without calling ahead.

What Was Changed: Prominently highlighted the mandatory "Call Before Traveling" requirement and on-call nature of the clinic across primary header banners and hero sections.

Impact: Prevents patient disappointment and ensures Dr. Ahmed Sarhan is notified and prepared at the clinic prior to the patient's arrival.

Fix 2: Directly Emphasized the Key Business Differentiator (Direct Access to Dentist)

Previous Issue: The direct communication channel with the principal dentist was not immediately obvious, leaving users unsure if they would reach a call center, front desk, or medical practitioner.

What Was Changed: Integrated explicit messaging around the emergency line (+971 58 505 8058 and WhatsApp https://wa.me/971585058058), explaining that calls connect directly to Dr. Ahmed Sarhan rather than a general reception switchboard.

Impact: Builds immediate trust for distressed patients seeking rapid, professional advice in English or Arabic during acute dental emergencies.

Fix 3: Categorized Emergency, General, and Cosmetic Services with Location Coverage

Previous Issue: Specialized emergency treatments and geographical coverage were not systematically organized, making quick navigation difficult during urgent situations.

What Was Changed: Structured core offerings into distinct service lists (Emergency Care, Severe Toothache, Root Canals, Abscesses, Broken Teeth, Knocked-Out Teeth, Wisdom Teeth, Lost Fillings/Crowns, General, and Cosmetic Dentistry) while explicitly listing covered areas (Barsha Heights, Dubai Marina, JBR, JLT, Palm Jumeirah, Business Bay, etc.).

Impact: Accelerates user orientation and confirms service suitability for patients looking for rapid local care across Dubai.

3. Design & User Experience Improvements

Visual Presentation & Palette: Designed a crisp, reassuring, high-contrast visual interface geared for immediate readability in high-stress, late-night, or urgent scenarios.

Layout & Information Architecture: Positioned critical call-to-actions—direct phone dialers (+971 58 505 8058) and WhatsApp initiation buttons—in sticky, high-visibility header and floating action positions.

User Experience & Accessibility: Elevated essential operational details, including 24/7/365 availability, precise building landmark info (Ground floor, 24 Hours Coffee building, Barsha Heights), and credential verification (DHA-licensed, BDS, 20+ years experience), to build instant credibility.
