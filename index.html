When you need urgent care

What kind of dental emergency are you experiencing?

Dental problems can happen unexpectedly. Whether you have severe tooth pain, a damaged tooth or swelling, contact the clinic to discuss the problem and arrange an appointment with Dr Sarhan.

Discuss Your Emergency

Severe Toothache

Persistent or intense tooth pain that needs prompt dental assessment.

Dental Abscess or Swelling

Dental infection concerns, gum swelling or facial swelling requiring urgent attention.

Broken or Chipped Tooth

Contact the clinic about a cracked, chipped or broken tooth following an accident.

Knocked-Out Tooth

Get in touch as soon as possible if a tooth has been knocked out.

Wisdom Tooth Pain

Discuss pain or discomfort associated with wisdom teeth with the dentist.

Lost Fillings or Crowns

Get advice if a filling, crown or veneer becomes loose, damaged or lost.

Not sure whether your problem is an emergency?

Call Dr Sarhan and explain what is happening before arranging your visit.
